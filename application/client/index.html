THE WORKSPACE GUIDE

A little guidance.
A smoother sales day.

CALLA I MENJA helps company sales representatives record and review customer orders. Order data and calculations are handled by the server.

01

Sign in

Enter your assigned username and password. Both fields are required. If your credentials cannot be verified, an error appears on the home screen.

02

Create a new order

Click “Create a new order” in the operations menu. Enter a unique order ID and the customer’s full name, physical address, email, and phone number. Check each product to enable its quantity, then enter a whole number from 1 to 10,000.

Select “Save order” to save the record. The server calculates and displays the total including 21% VAT. Product prices exclude VAT.

03

Review your orders

Click “View orders” in the operations menu. Choose “View all orders” or “View one order by ID”, then click “Continue”. For one order, enter its exact ID on the next screen and click “Find order”. Tables show customer details, products, quantities, prices, subtotal, VAT, total, and creation details.

If no file or matching order is available, an error page provides navigation back to the menus.
